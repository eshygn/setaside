# SetAside: voice-over for ElevenLabs

Paste **only the text inside the box** into ElevenLabs as one piece. The blank lines between paragraphs give natural pauses. The recorded video (`setaside-demo.mp4`, 2:40) is paced to this text at a normal reading speed (ElevenLabs speed 1.0). The table shows where each paragraph starts, so you can nudge it if the voice runs fast or slow.

```
If you get paid through PayPal on the side, for prints, freelance work or content, your business income lands in the same account as pizza money and rent splits. Nobody tells you what's yours to spend, until the tax bill arrives. This is SetAside.

SetAside reads PayPal in Transaction Search's format, thirty-one days at a time. For this demo, that's a year of fictional history. Then Gemini sorts every transaction: sale, client payment, refund, fee, personal transfer or reimbursement, each with a confidence score and a reason. In this recording, the answers are replayed from real Gemini runs, and the app says so in the corner.

Twenty-two it isn't sure about, and it says so. Kofi is a friend who pays Jo back with goods and services, so one payment looked like a sale. I fix it once. SetAside turns that into a rule, and eighteen similar payments update on their own. Ruth's share of the bills is a reimbursement, not income. Six more follow.

It all lands in an AG Studio dashboard with three custom widgets: a tax pot showing what belongs to the tax office, the quarters and deadlines, and where the money came from. Click a category, and the whole page filters.

And Jo can just ask. Show refunds against sales by month. Our custom agent, running inside AG Studio's agent framework, builds the chart live. What if I'd charged ten percent more? It redraws the tax widgets: about twelve hundred pounds more tax, but around thirteen hundred more kept.

A client paid without an invoice? SetAside raises one and records the payment against it. In this sandbox, the invoicing step runs on a mock, and the app says so. And clients can pay Jo with real PayPal buttons, created through the Orders API.

Jump the clock to two weeks before the January deadline. New activity arrives and sorts itself, following Jo's rules. Jo knows exactly what to put aside, and what's safe to spend.

No spreadsheet. No shock in January. SetAside turns a messy PayPal account into a number you can trust. Built with PayPal, Gemini and AG Studio.
```
